## The entry

`main.py` writes one one-line office joke to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Our fifteen-minute standup takes fourteen minutes of Dave
> explaining why his "quick question" needs a slide deck,
> a spreadsheet, and a second meeting.

The joke is built for the judge brief: a genuine office-life joke
(the daily standup), a turn at the end (the "quick question" that
needs a deck, a spreadsheet, and another meeting), and recognition
every office worker knows — the standup that is really one person
talking.

## Running

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes the joke to /output/entry.txt
```
